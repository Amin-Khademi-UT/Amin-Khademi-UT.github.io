About Me

Academic background and research profile

I am an Environmental Engineer with an MSc in Environmental Engineering (Air Pollution) from the University of Tehran. My academic and research experience focuses on understanding atmospheric processes and environmental pollution through mathematical modelling, computational methods and observational data analysis.

My work combines environmental engineering with particular interests in greenhouse gas emissions, atmospheric dispersion, satellite observations, air quality assessment and machine learning applications in environmental research.

MSc Research

Graduate research and computational methods

Methane Emission Estimation and Atmospheric Modelling

My MSc research investigated methane emissions from landfill sites using environmental modelling, satellite observations and computational analysis.

The research involved atmospheric dispersion modelling, evaluation of methane emission estimation approaches, comparison with satellite-based observations and exploration of data-driven forecasting methods.

Relevant methods and tools included AERMOD, EPA LandGEM, satellite observations and Python-based analysis.
